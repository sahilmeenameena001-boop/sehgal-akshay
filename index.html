About CHAMBERS OF AKSHAY SEHGAL

Cheque Dishonour Litigation in Delhi

The practice focuses on cheque dishonour proceedings and related criminal remedies, from the initial complaint through appellate review.

The chambers handles Section 138 matters before the Delhi District Courts, including proceedings at Patiala House Courts, and related matters before the High Court and Supreme Court of India.

The practice draws on the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, and the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, as applicable to the proceeding.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions

What matters does Akshay Sehgal handle?

He represents clients in cheque dishonour complaints under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act and in related criminal proceedings.

Where does he appear?

His practice includes the Delhi District Courts, including Patiala House Courts, the High Court and the Supreme Court of India.

What can I bring to an initial discussion?

Bring the cheque, bank return memo, any notices or replies, and any complaint, summons or order you have received.

Does he handle High Court petitions?

Yes. His practice includes quashing and miscellaneous petitions arising from cheque dishonour proceedings.

Does he handle bail applications?

Yes. He handles bail applications and related requests for interim relief.

Does he handle criminal appeals and revisions?

Yes. His practice covers appeals and revisions before the High Court and criminal appeals before the Supreme Court of India.

Which statutes feature in the practice?

The Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, and the applicable criminal procedure under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, or the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973.
